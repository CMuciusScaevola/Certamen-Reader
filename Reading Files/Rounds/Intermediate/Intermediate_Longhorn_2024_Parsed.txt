ANSWER:DECIUS BONUS: What emperor, who owned a pet chicken named Roma, moved the capital to Ravenna and reigned when Rome was sacked in 410 AD? ANSWER:HONORIUS  
“Horrifying in his terrible squalor, a mass of white beard lies unkempt on his chin, his eyes glow with a steady flame.” describes what character in the Underworld, whom the Cumaean Sibyl shows a golden branch in lieu of an obol, the typical requirement to get on his ferry? ANSWER:CHARON  BONUS: While talking to Charon, Aeneas spots what helmsman of his, who, because he was put to sleep at the helm by Somnus, fell overboard and was killed off the coast of Italy? ANSWER:PALINURUS  BONUS: Before he is shown the golden branch, Charon is very hesitant to take a living hero across, citing how what two living men previously caused trouble in the Underworld when they attempted to carry off Persephone? ANSWER:THESEUS and PIRITHOUS
